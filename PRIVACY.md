# Privacy

Effective September 1, 2026

Motif is a downloadable molecular-biology workbench. This policy describes the
current open-source distribution. Motif does not currently operate a hosted
service.

## Data Motif processes

Motif processes the records and supporting material that you choose to open,
including DNA, RNA, protein, annotations, alignments, notes, analysis results,
and provenance. The local MCP server and workbench process that material on
your computer.

Motif does not intentionally send this material to a Motif-operated server.
Networking is off by default in the current distribution. Motif does not
include advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies.

## AI hosts and other software

You may use Motif through Codex, Claude Science, another compatible MCP host,
or a web browser. Information that you provide to those products is governed
by their terms, privacy policies, organization settings, and data controls.
Motif cannot control how a host handles prompts, attachments, tool inputs, or
tool results.

External analysis programs, browsers, operating systems, package managers, and
source-control services have their own data practices. Review them before
using Motif with sensitive material.

## Local storage and exported files

Motif may store display preferences and session state in browser storage on
your computer. You can reset display preferences from the workbench.

Portable HTML, Database JSON, workspace ZIP, FASTA, GenBank, CLUSTAL, CSV, and
other exports are ordinary files. They are not encrypted by Motif. Anyone who
can read those files may be able to read the biological records and notes they
contain. You are responsible for choosing a safe location, applying suitable
access controls, and deleting copies you no longer need.

## Diagnostics and support

Motif's diagnostic tools are designed to report versions, integrity results,
counts, and bounded error information without echoing complete sequences or
credentials. Before sharing a report, inspect it and remove private paths,
identifiers, unpublished biological data, credentials, connector
configuration, and other sensitive information.

For public support requests, reproduce problems with a small public or
synthetic record whenever possible.

## Sensitive and regulated data

Motif is not presented as a regulated clinical system or as secure storage for
protected health information. Do not use Motif with confidential, personal,
controlled, or regulated data unless you have authority to do so and have
independently determined that your complete environment—including the MCP host
and exported-file storage—meets your obligations.

## Hosted services

Motif does not currently offer a hosted MCP endpoint or user account service.
If that changes, this policy will be updated before the hosted service is made
available and will describe collection, retention, deletion, subprocessors,
security, and user choices for that service.

## Changes and questions

Material changes will be recorded in the repository history and reflected by
the effective date above. Privacy questions may be opened through the
[Motif support tracker](https://github.com/jvogan/motif/issues). Do not include
sensitive data in a public issue.
